ECSAtech

We bring ECSA members together to explore responsible uses of artificial intelligence (AI) in citizen science, develop practical resources and discuss how citizen science data can connect with the European Green Deal data space.

Take part

ECSA members can contribute during sessions and between them:

  • Share examples from citizen science projects.
  • Suggest or demonstrate tools and describe what you learned from using them.
  • Comment on draft principles and help develop shared resources.
  • Bring questions about data sharing and how different systems can exchange and use citizen science data.

Session details and participation information will be shared through ECSA working group channels.
