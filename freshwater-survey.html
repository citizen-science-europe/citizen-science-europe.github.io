Follow the workflow demonstrated in the seminar

  1. Open New observation. Enter a test site name and add or confirm its coordinates.
  2. Enter the observation date and optional time. Add photographs, notes, participant counts or observations about the waterbody, surroundings, flow, visible conditions and water quality as appropriate.
  3. Save the observation. The presenter saw a pending upload on a weak connection; the session did not establish that the full workflow worked offline.
  4. Open the shared map and select a marker. Use the records view to inspect submissions and export a CSV.
  5. Compare public viewing with owner editing. In the demo, owner sign-in enabled editing and photograph removal.

Other trials and what to verify

An earlier ChatGPT build used a temporary address. Z.ai published a tabbed prototype after about 80 minutes, with sample observations around Oxfordshire. Grok hit a free-tier limit; DeepSeek and Perplexity did not produce builds in the reported trials. These were individual trials under different conditions.

Before adapting the prototype for field use, confirm where data and images are stored, which fields can be public, how queued submissions behave and who maintains the site. Test whether shared records persist across devices and whether the CSV retains every required field. The build prompts describe requirements; the demonstration did not verify all of them.
