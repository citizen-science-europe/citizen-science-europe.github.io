Projects, data, tools and technology working group

This working group aims to build a shared understanding of interests in the citizen science data space by examining challenges that citizen science projects face around data, including interoperability, reliability, privacy and intellectual property rights. It works with technical organisations such as the Open Geospatial Consortium and other citizen science associations such as CSA and ACSA.

Activity plan

The ECSA working group page lists these priorities:

  • Spearhead the sharing of resources (projects, data, tools and technologies) for citizen science by providing examples and developing best practices.
  • Intensify collaborations with the CSA working group, especially to promote international standards for citizen science data and metadata.
  • Contribute to a newly proposed citizen science initiative for GEO.
  • Contribute to wrapping up the OGC interoperability experiment on citizen science.
  • Collaborate with The Things Network, a community building a worldwide open, decentralised Internet of Things.

Past achievements

The working group has co-organised and attended meetings and events, including a workshop on citizen science standards in June 2018, the OGC Citizen Science Domain Working Group meeting in September 2018, CitSci 2019 and a workshop on improving data standardisation and interoperability in March 2019.

Its contributions include the Geneva Declaration on Citizen Science Data and Metadata Standards, a proposal for an update of PPRS-Core, input to a proposed GEO initiative on citizen science, an initial demonstrator for a citizen science project explorer and an early connectivity demonstration of DIY instruments with The Things Network.
